# VIctory-Navi10 v0.2

- Milestone 0. Local branch `v0.2-meshlets` starts at alpha commit `a6e8c78`. Previous raw benchmarks and the separate GPL workspace are preserved. The alpha compiles. The earlier stable executable passed 12 exact image comparisons and 90 timed frames on the RX 5700 XT. Driver UMD `32.0.21045.29003`, Windows build `26300`.
- Milestone 1 validated. `--test-meshlets` adds a synthetic indexed triangle path alongside the city. All 24 fixtures passed on both the RX 5700 XT and WARP, including exact RGBA, index triples, indirect arguments, vertex contents, capacity counters and canaries. Sixteen runs used 128 concurrent threadgroups competing for one triangle. The final build also passed 12 city image comparisons and 90 timed regression frames.
- Fix and measurements. HLSL 5.1 evaluated a reservation before rejecting vertex overflow, which could expose unwritten indices to the draw. Check vertex capacity before calling the reservation. RX 5700 XT compute timestamps ranged from 0.00236 to 0.00644 ms for the fixtures, including transitions after dispatch. Local JSON and CSV are in `runs/triangle-v02/`. These small fixture timings describe correctness tests.
- Windows. CreateProcess returned Win32 4551 with Smart App Control enabled. After the user changed the setting, local execution succeeded. The exact enforcement rule remains unconfirmed.
- Upstream PR #961 is open at `31568ced3e44bb31aac5755e18c7d6f165204e30`. Its source diff shows separate vertex, index and primitive outputs and a generated vertex reader. Its reported tests and timings are upstream results. Navi10 uses its own synthetic input format and a bounded index stream. [PR #961](https://github.com/Marvisak/VIctory/pull/961/files).
- Milestone 2 validated. Vertices carry interpolated color and normals. The material stream stores one flat 32-bit ID per emitted triangle, addressed by `SV_PrimitiveID`. All 26 fixtures and both shading controls passed on RX 5700 XT and WARP. The final build passed 12 city comparisons and 90 timed regression frames. Raw fixture results are in `runs/attributes-radeon/`.
- Next. Milestone 3 adds perspective projection and conservative clipping in homogeneous coordinates.
